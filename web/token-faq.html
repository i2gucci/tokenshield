TOKEN & GOVERNANCE FAQ

This page answers common questions about the Token Shield governance token and its upcoming pump.fun launch.

Transparency First: We believe in honest communication. This page explains why we're launching a token, what it does, and what it doesn't do. No hype, no promises, just facts.

$ What is the Token Shield governance token?

Token Shield will launch a governance token via pump.fun in Q2 2026 as a fair launch community distribution mechanism. The token provides:

  • Governance Rights: Vote on protocol parameters, oracle selection, risk models, and eligible token lists
  • Premium Discounts: 5-20% off insurance premiums based on holdings (tiered)
  • LP Priority Access: Token holders get priority allocation to insurance pool LP positions
  • Staking Rewards: 10% of protocol premium revenue distributed to stakers

This is a utility token with real functionality, not a speculative fundraising mechanism.

$ Why launch on pump.fun?

Philosophical Alignment:

  • Fair Launch: No presale, no VC allocation, no insider advantages. Everyone buys the same bonding curve.
  • Community First: Token holders ARE the community. They're financially committed from day one.
  • Permissionless: Aligns with Solana's ethos. No gatekeepers, no approval needed.
  • Market Validation: Bonding curve completion proves genuine community demand, not VC interests.

Who We're Building For:

Token Shield insures degen meme traders. Launching on pump.fun (the epicenter of degen culture) means our community is aligned from the start. If you believe insurance for meme coins is needed, you can participate. If not, you can watch from the sidelines.

$ Is this how you're funding development?

No. Let's be crystal clear:

Pump.fun Creator Fee: 6 SOL (~$510 at current prices) - One-time payment when bonding curve completes

What $510 Covers: Gas fees, initial marketing, community incentives

What $510 Does NOT Cover: Audits ($40-50k), developer salaries, oracle integration, legal fees

Actual Funding Sources:

  • Solana Foundation Grant: $40-60k (applied, emphasizing public goods + innovation)
  • Founder Investment: $25k personal capital (skin in the game)
  • Team Shield Revenue: $15-25k from pilot customers before token launch
  • Protocol Revenue: Insurance premiums fund ongoing operations once live
  • Pump.fun Fee: $510 (symbolic, helps with gas/marketing)

The token launch is about community alignment and fair distribution, not primary funding. We're not relying on token sales to build this protocol.

$ What does the token actually do?

Phase 1: Launch Day (Q2 2026)

  • Governance Voting: Participate in protocol decisions via Snapshot/Realms
  • Community Access: Token-gated Discord channels, early docs access
  • Proposal Rights: Submit governance proposals with 10,000+ token holdings

Phase 2: Protocol Beta (Q3 2026)

  • Premium Discounts: Tiered discounts on insurance premiums
    • 1k-10k tokens: 5% discount
    • 10k-50k tokens: 10% discount
    • 50k-100k tokens: 15% discount
    • 100k+ tokens: 20% discount + priority claims
  • Non-Custodial: Checked at policy creation, you keep tokens in your wallet

Phase 3: Mainnet (Q4 2026)

  • Staking Rewards: 10% of all protocol revenue → staking pool (distributed in USDC)
  • Revenue Sources: Insurance premiums + yield earnings from pool deployment (expect 35-50% revenue boost from yield)
  • LP Priority: Token holders get priority allocation to insurance pool LP slots
  • Buyback Program: 5% of quarterly profits used to buy + burn tokens

Phase 4: Long-Term (2027+)

  • Exclusive Products: Advanced coverage types reserved for holders
  • Revenue Share: Additional profit sharing as protocol matures
  • DAO Control: Community owns and operates the protocol

Key Point: This is a cash-flowing utility token. Staking rewards come from real protocol revenue (insurance premiums + yield strategies), not inflation or ponzi mechanics. The pool generates 4-7% APY by deploying reserves to battle-tested protocols like Marinade, Jito, and Marginfi. The protocol generates revenue, holders capture value. (APY rates as of Feb 28, 2026)

$ Common concerns & honest answers

"This looks like a cash grab"

We understand the skepticism. Pump.fun has a reputation. Here's our response:

  • We're collecting $510, not $100k+ in VC funding
  • Real funding comes from grants ($40-60k) and Team Shield business revenue
  • Team tokens locked 12 months (provable on-chain)
  • GitHub is public - you can verify we're building
  • If we rug, token goes to zero. We have more to lose than anyone.

"Why not just build first, token later?"

Valid question. Two reasons:

  1. Community from Day One: Early supporters who take risk alongside us deserve upside participation. Launching after mainnet means VCs/insiders got all the early allocation.
  2. Aligned Incentives: Token holders become protocol advocates. They're incentivized to provide feedback, spread awareness, and drive insurance adoption.

"Team could still dump after vesting"

True, but consider:

  • We're doxxed (LinkedIn profiles, GitHub accounts public)
  • If protocol succeeds, team allocation is worth more long-term
  • Dumping tanks the token + our reputation + future in crypto
  • Vesting unlocks are tied to milestones (beta, mainnet, TVL targets)
  • DAO can vote to extend vesting if community wants

"What if the protocol fails?"

Then token goes to zero, same as any failed project. We're not promising success. We're promising:

  • Honest effort and transparent development
  • Public documentation of all decisions
  • Smart contracts will be audited before mainnet
  • Community ownership of the protocol via DAO

Risk is inherent. Token reflects that risk.

"Pump.fun tokens usually die"

You're right. Most do. Here's what's different:

  • We have a product roadmap (not just a meme)
  • Token has real utility (discounts, governance, staking rewards)
  • Dual revenue streams: insurance premiums + yield strategies (4-7% APY on pool reserves as of Feb 2026)
  • Team is public and accountable
  • Fair launch means no early dumpers

We're not pretending this isn't risky. It is. But it's not a pure speculation play.

$ Token launch timeline & criteria

Pre-Launch Milestones (Required):

  • ✅ Website live with documentation
  • ⏳ Smart contracts 80%+ complete (testnet deployed)
  • ⏳ Stress tests showing economic viability
  • ⏳ Legal entity formed (DAO LLC or Foundation)
  • ⏳ Primary funding secured ($65k+ from grants + founder + pilot revenue)
  • ⏳ Team comfortable with public scrutiny (doxxing)
  • ⏳ Crisis communication plan prepared

Tentative Launch: Q2 2026

Launch Process:

  1. Week -2: Public announcement, detailed tokenomics disclosure
  2. Week -1: Team doxxing (LinkedIn, GitHub), open Q&A
  3. Day 0: Token creation on pump.fun, bonding curve opens
  4. Day 1-7: Community buying on curve, transparent communication
  5. Day 7+: If curve completes → Raydium LP creation, DEX trading begins
  6. Week 2+: Weekly updates on holder count, volume, development progress

Go/No-Go Decision Factors:

We will NOT launch if:

  • Primary funding not secured (need $65k+ committed from grants/pilot/founder)
  • Smart contracts major bugs/vulnerabilities discovered
  • Legal consultation indicates regulatory risk
  • Market conditions extremely adverse (SOL crash, regulatory crackdown)
  • Team not ready for public scrutiny

Quality Over Speed: We will delay launch if criteria aren't met. Rushing to market with a half-baked token helps no one. We'd rather build credibility through patience than hype through FOMO.

$ Bottom line: Should I buy the token?

That's entirely up to you. Here's our honest take:

Buy the token if:

  • You believe insurance for meme coins is needed and will be adopted
  • You want governance rights and premium discounts
  • You're comfortable with high risk (this is very early stage)
  • You want to support fair launch DeFi vs VC-backed projects
  • You understand the token could go to zero

Don't buy the token if:

  • You're looking for guaranteed returns (there are none)
  • You can't afford to lose the money
  • You're not interested in governance/utility, just speculation
  • You're skeptical about the project's viability
  • You think all pump.fun tokens are scams

Our Commitment:

Whether you buy the token or not, we're building this protocol. The token is a way to involve the community early and create aligned incentives. If it doesn't work, we'll pivot. The insurance protocol doesn't live or die based on token performance.

Final Word:

We're not trying to convince you to buy. We're trying to be transparent about what we're building, why we're doing it this way, and what the risks are. Make your own decision. DYOR. Not financial advice.
